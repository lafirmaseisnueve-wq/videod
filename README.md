# Ultimate Music Video Director

By thefirm media studios and gemini ai. A professional AI-powered music video storyboard and creative direction studio.

## Features

- **Audio Analysis:** Transcribe lyrics and map emotional arcs from uploaded audio.
- **AI Storyboard Generation:** Automatically generate a visual shot list with technical camera directions.
- **Reference Assets:** Generate character identities, costumes, and props as visual "ingredients".
- **Cinematic Visuals:** High-quality image generation for each scene.
- **Exportable Storyboards:** Export your complete creative brief and shot list to PDF.

## Getting Started

1. Upload your music track (MP3/WAV).
2. Define your creative vision and select a director style.
3. Let the AI Director build your storyboard.
4. Export and use as a master reference for your production.
